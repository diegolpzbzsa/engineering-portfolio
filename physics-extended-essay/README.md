\# Physics Extended Essay



\## Energy Transfer in a Drum



This project was my IB Physics Extended Essay, an independent research project in which I investigated energy transfer and damping in a vibrating drum membrane.



I was interested in understanding how a physical system that appears relatively simple can actually involve several interacting variables. The project required me to turn the theoretical physics into an experiment that I could measure, analyze, and compare with mathematical models.



\## What I worked on



\- Designed the experimental method

\- Collected and analyzed experimental data

\- Applied mathematical and physical models to the system

\- Studied energy transfer and damping in an oscillating membrane

\- Evaluated experimental uncertainty and limitations

\- Adjusted the analysis when the real system did not behave exactly as the theoretical model predicted



\## What I learned



The most important part of this project was learning that solving an engineering or physics problem is not only about finding the correct equation. A model makes assumptions, while a real experiment introduces uncertainty, measurement limitations, and behavior that may not be captured perfectly.



Because of this, I had to understand what each part of the model represented physically and decide which conclusions were actually supported by the data.



This way of thinking is one of the reasons I became increasingly interested in experimental engineering and control systems.
