I enjoy working on problems where physics and mathematical models have to be connected with real systems through design, testing, and iteration.



\## Projects



\### \[Physics Extended Essay — Energy Transfer in a Drum](physics-extended-essay/)



Independent physics research investigating energy transfer and damping in a vibrating drum membrane.



The project required me to turn a theoretical physics problem into an experiment that could be measured and analyzed. I designed the experimental approach, collected and analyzed data, applied mathematical and physical models, and evaluated the limitations of the results.



\### Purdue Space Program — Active Controls



Member of the Active Controls team working on guidance, navigation, and control for experimental rocket systems.



My current work involves developing my understanding of rigid-body dynamics, attitude representation, sensors, state estimation, and control-system modeling.



\### \[Experimental Rocket Propulsion Prototype](rocket-propulsion-prototype/)



Worked on the design, development, and testing of an experimental rocket propulsion prototype.



The project involved moving from initial design decisions into physical testing, analyzing the results, and modifying the system based on experimental behavior.
